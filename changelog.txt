Version: 1.1.21
Date: 27 November 2026
  Changes:
    - Removed the bundled tf_util library inherited from Klonan's mods; the mod now uses Factorio's built-in util and no longer adds its own functions to it, so other mods that ship the same library (e.g. Teleporters) can't swap in their versions. Thanks to stef77stef
  Bugfixes:
    - Fix failing to load alongside mods that also define a global named "shared" (e.g. Mining Drones Remastered): data-final-fixes.lua could pick up the other mod's table and crash with "attempt to index field 'units'"
    - Fix the drone and repair toggle hotkeys starting unbound on Factorio 2.1 (unknown key_sequence "SHIFT + ]" / "SHIFT + ["); they now default to SHIFT + ] and SHIFT + [ again
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 1.1.20
Date: 15 November 2026
  Changes:
    - Drone quality stats now scale with the quality's level, so qualities added by other mods get drones too
    - Batch sizes adapt to the number of free drones: with drones to spare every target gets its own drone, and drones only carry several targets when there are more targets than drones
    - Higher quality drones carry bigger batches and are dispatched first; drones are no longer picked at random
    - Rebalanced quality stats. Each quality level adds 20% speed, 30% health and 2 inventory slots, up to level 10:
        - Uncommon: speed 0.24 (was 0.3), 58 health (was 60), 6 slots
        - Rare: speed 0.28 (was 0.6), 72 health (was 75), 8 slots
        - Epic: speed 0.32 (was 0.8), 85 health (was 120), 10 slots (was 12)
        - Legendary: speed 0.4 (was 1.2), 112 health (was 240), 14 slots (was 16)
  Bugfixes:
    - Fix spectral drones colliding with buildings like normal drones
    - Fix drones looking busy for a new player who took over a removed player's slot
    - Fix drones standing on the ghost they were building (or on each other's ghosts) and never building it; drones standing still in a ghost's footprint are moved aside again, while drones just walking through are still left alone
    - Fix drones building ghosts that Construction Approvals had un-approved: drones now re-check a ghost before building it, skip unapproved ghosts when chaining builds after deconstruction, and no longer pull nearby ghosts/upgrades/deconstructions from other forces or players into a batch
